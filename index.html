03 / A comprehensive mission editor

From a chart to
your next session.

Eight guided steps. The freedom to configure the exercise yourself. Start with bundled example missions, then build a collection that fits your practice.

01

Bring your own approach

Import a PDF or image and select the chart page.

02

Put your chart in scale

Orient the chart, calibrate by distance, latitude or longitude, and set magnetic variation.

03

Build your navigation references

Place, name and move multiple beacons. Set optional beacon-specific magnetic variation.

04

Connect the instruments

Choose your instruments, associate them independently with beacons, and configure DME ground or slant range.

05

Set up the next flight

Place the aircraft, set its heading, choose fixed or random wind, then name and save your mission.

Make it repeatable

A mission you can
come back to.

Keep your chart, navigation references and starting conditions together. Save the setup once, then revisit the exercise whenever you want to practise.
