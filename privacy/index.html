Privacy Policy

Effective date: 26 October 2026

Real Approach Trainer operates entirely offline and does not collect personal information.

Data collection and accounts

The app does not collect, transmit, track or share personal data with the developer. No user account is required. The app makes no network requests and uses no advertising, analytics, crash-reporting SDKs or third-party tracking services.

Data on your device

Missions, settings and imported charts are stored locally on your device.

Sharing mission files

You can explicitly export or share mission files, including their charts, through iOS sharing functionality. You choose where to send them. The developer does not receive these files unless you independently choose to send them to us.

Contact

If you contact us by email, we receive the email address and information you choose to send. For privacy questions, contact grekonstudio@gmail.com.

Policy updates

This policy may be updated if the app’s data-handling practices change. Any updated policy will be published on this page with a revised effective date.
