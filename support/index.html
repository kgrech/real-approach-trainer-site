Bug Reports

Need help or have an idea? Email grekonstudio@gmail.com.

Report a bug

Describe what happened and what you expected to happen. Please include:

  • The app version.
  • Your device model.
  • Your operating-system version.
  • The steps needed to reproduce the problem, and how often it happens.

You may attach screenshots or a screen recording if they help explain the issue.

Email a bug report ↗

Request a feature

Email your suggestion with the subject “Real Approach Trainer feature request”. Explain what you would like to do and how the feature would help your training.

Before you send

Please do not include passwords or sensitive personal information. Check any screenshots, recordings and attachments before sharing them.

Real Approach Trainer is a two-dimensional instrument-navigation practice tool. It does not automatically reproduce or validate every element of a published approach, and is not intended for real-world navigation or as a substitute for flight instruction.
